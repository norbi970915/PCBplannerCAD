### Selection and view
- Selection filter (types + layers, "active layer only", ratsnest toggle), from the toolbar funnel, layer tabs and View panel. The layer filter persists. An empty box selection caused by the filter offers "Reset filter".
- Shift+S cycles the layer display: all → dimmed → active only (Ctrl+S stays Save).
- Net highlight: Alt+click or ` marks a net and dims other copper; I selects the net's copper; Esc clears the highlight first.
- **U** selects the segment chain up to the next pad/via/junction; a second U selects the whole connection.
- **Ctrl+F Find**: footprints/parts by designator, value or footprint; nets, labels, texts; `*` `?` wildcards; works in schematics and boards.

### Placement
- Right-click rotates 90° and middle-click flips a part while moving it.
- Dragging a routed part keeps its tracks: Preserve angles (the pad segment slides at its angle along its neighbour), Free, or Disconnect (Tab). Shift+Tab sets what re-routed connections do with other nets: shove, walk around, ignore. Other parts never move. Core: `crates/core/src/pcb/drag.rs`.
- Dragging a routed track segment keeps the neighbours at their angles; pad/via ends get a joining stub (`app/src/editor/trackDrag.ts`).
- Align left/right/top/bottom/centres and distribute horizontally/vertically (Edit ▸ Align / Distribute, context menu).
- **Pack & Move (P P)**: packs the selection into tight rows and floats it on the cursor; routed parts stay connected (`drag::drag_each`).
- New footprints from Update PCB (D U) arrive on the cursor; Esc leaves them beside the board.
- Locked footprints refuse M, R and drags.
- Unroute (Edit menu / context menu): removes a part's routing up to the first junction, pour or other pad.

### Routing
- Routing starts on the clicked pad's layer and switches the active layer.
- While routing, the other ends of the net get cyan rings plus a guide line to the nearest. W / Shift+W cycle widths; * or V adds a via.
- Via types (through, blind, buried, microvia) per layer pair in the layer stack manager; the router picks them and DRC accepts them.

### New tools
- **Cleanup Tracks & Vias**: merge collinear, redundant, zero-length, dangling, vias on fewer than two layers (`pcb/cleanup.rs`).
- **Via Stitching**: grid or staggered vias in a pour, DRC-checked (`pcb/stitch.rs`).
- **Teardrops**: zones flagged `teardrop` (solid, top priority) for vias, PTH and near-square SMD pads, curved or straight; imported teardrop zones retain their purpose. Teardrops whose track or pad goes are removed in the same undo step (`pcb/teardrop.rs`, `Session::execute`, `Route`).
- **Dimensions (P D)**: aligned / horizontal / vertical, live value in mm / mil / inch, editable in Properties, plotted to Gerber and IPC-2581; aligned and orthogonal dimensions import (`pcb/dimension.rs`, `app/src/editor/dimension.ts`).
- **Board Statistics & Drill Table** (copy as CSV).
- Layer stack manager draws a scaled cross-section including via spans.
- Nets panel: Vias column and routed length per layer for the selected net.

### Design rules
- **Rules on/off** (Ctrl+Shift+D, toolbar shield, status bar): off makes routing, diff pairs and part moves ignore violators; the chosen modes come back when rules go on.
- **Online DRC** (on by default, status bar / Tools menu): after every change, including undo/redo, only the changed objects are re-checked (`drc::run_near`) and their markers replaced. Opening a board runs one full check. DRC commands run asynchronously on a document snapshot, so editing is not blocked. Unrouted connections stay in the ratsnest and the full T D check.
- DRC error markers get a dark halo and a white ring so they read on red top copper.
